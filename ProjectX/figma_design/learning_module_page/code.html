Conducting Effective User Interviews

In this lesson, we dive deep into the qualitative research method of user interviews. You will learn how to prepare a discussion guide, ask non-leading questions, and make the user feel comfortable enough to share their honest experiences.

Key Takeaways

  • check Structure your interview with a clear warm-up, body, and cool-down phase.
  • check Avoid "Yes/No" questions; focus on "How" and "Why".
  • check Learn to embrace silence to let the user think and elaborate.
