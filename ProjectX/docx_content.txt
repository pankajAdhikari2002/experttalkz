▶ DISCIPLINE: CHEMICAL ENGINEERING
Course: Offshore Process Engineering
Master the fundamentals and advanced techniques of offshore process design, from concept to commissioning.
What you'll learn:
Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs) and P&IDs
HYSYS / Aspen PLUS simulations
Heat & mass balance calculations
Relief valve sizing and flare system design
Process safety — HAZOP, HAZID, SIL
Offshore topsides process design
Ideal For
Chemical engineers, process engineers, fresh graduates targeting O&G sector
▶ DISCIPLINE: INSTRUMENTATION ENGINEERING
Course: Offshore Instrumentation & Control Systems
Develop deep expertise in measurement, control, and safety systems used on offshore platforms worldwide.
What you'll learn:
Instrumentation engineering basics and offshore applications
DCS, PLC, and SCADA systems
SIL/SIS design and assessment
Control valve sizing and selection
Cause & effect diagrams
Hook-up drawings and instrument data sheets
Ideal For
Instrumentation engineers, automation specialists, electrical engineers seeking cross-discipline knowledge
▶ DISCIPLINE: MECHANICAL ENGINEERING
Course: Offshore Piping Stress Engineering (CAESAR II)
One of our most sought-after programs — a complete deep-dive into piping stress analysis for offshore and onshore projects.
What you'll learn:
Piping system design fundamentals
CAESAR II modelling and analysis
ASME B31.3 / B31.4 / B31.8 codes
Static and dynamic stress analysis
Nozzle load assessment
Piping isometrics and drawings
Course: Rotating Equipment Engineering
Pumps, compressors, turbines — understand selection, sizing, troubleshooting, and API standards.
Ideal For
Mechanical engineers, piping designers, plant engineers in Oil & Gas
▶ DISCIPLINE: ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING
Course: Offshore Electrical Engineering
A comprehensive program covering HV/LV power systems, protection, and electrical area classification for offshore environments.
What you'll learn:
Offshore power generation and distribution

Electrical area classification (IEC 60079)
Motor control centres (MCC) and switchgear
Cable sizing and selection
Hazardous area equipment standards
Ideal For
Electrical engineers transitioning into offshore/Oil & Gas roles
▶ DISCIPLINE: CIVIL & STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING
Course: Offshore Structural Engineering

Headline

The Expertalkz Oil & Gas Knowledge Hub is your always-on resource for industry intelligence — written and curated by working engineers. Whether you're a student, a professional, or a recruiter, this is where real O&G knowledge lives.

▶ SUB-SECTION 1: Concept to Commissioning
Intro
Ever wondered how an offshore platform goes from an idea on paper to a fully operational facility pumping millions of barrels? This series walks you through every stage — FEED, detailed engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning — with real-world context and insider insights.
What's inside:
Front End Engineering Design (FEED) — what it really means
How project phases connect across disciplines
Key deliverables at each stage
Common pitfalls engineers face and how to avoid them
Lessons from real-world O&G megaprojects
▶ SUB-SECTION 2: Oil & Gas Industry Overview
Intro
Before you can master your discipline, you need to understand the big picture. This overview covers how the global Oil & Gas industry is structured — from upstream exploration to midstream transport to downstream refining — and where your role fits in the puzzle.
Topics covered:
Upstream, midstream, and downstream explained
How offshore platforms are categorised (fixed, FPSO, semi-sub)
The economics of Oil & Gas — how projects get funded
Key players: operators, EPCs, contractors, and consultants
India's Oil & Gas sector: ONGC, Reliance, IOC and the future
▶ SUB-SECTION 3: Industry News & Trends
Intro
Stay sharp. Stay current. The O&G industry is evolving faster than ever — with energy transition, decarbonisation, and digital disruption reshaping every corner of the sector. Our news and trends section keeps you informed on what matters.
What we cover:
Global oil price movements and their impact on projects
LNG, hydrogen, and the energy transition
Digital twins, AI, and IoT on offshore platforms
New regulatory frameworks and environmental mandates
Emerging markets and investment hotspots in O&G
▶ SUB-SECTION 4: Piping System Components & Equipment
Intro
Piping is the circulatory system of any oil & gas facility. In this section, we break down every component — from flanges and valves to fittings and instruments — with engineering-grade explanations that prepare you for real project work.
Components covered:
Pipes, flanges, and fittings — materials, schedules, ratings
Valves: gate, globe, ball, butterfly, check, control
Pressure vessels and heat exchangers
Pumps and compressors — selection principles
Instrumentation taps, bypass loops, and drains/vents
▶ SUB-SECTION 5: Piping Design & Engineering
Intro
This is where theory meets practice. Our piping design section dives deep into the engineering discipline that underpins every plant and offshore facility — from layout fundamentals to stress analysis to 3D modelling.
Topics covered:
Piping layout and routing principles
Pipe stress analysis — when, why, and how
3D modelling: clash detection and isometric extraction
Pipe support types — selection and spacing
Line designation tables and piping specs
▶ SUB-SECTION 6: Standards, Codes & Compliance
Intro
Knowing the codes isn't optional — it's essential. This section is your reference library for the standards and regulations that govern engineering in the Oil & Gas industry. From ASME to API to IEC, we break them down in plain language.
Key codes covered:
ASME BPVC Section VIII — Pressure Vessel Design (Parts 1, 2, 3 — Hindi & English)
ASME B31.3 — Process Piping
API 650 / 620 — Storage Tanks
API RP 2A — Offshore Platform Design
IEC 60079 — Hazardous Area Classification
ISO 13703 — Offshore Piping Design
📝 Podcast series: History of ASME BPVC Section VIII — available in Hindi & English (Parts 1, 2, 3)
▶ SUB-SECTION 7: Latest Technologies in Oil & Gas
Intro
The oil field of tomorrow looks nothing like the one today. Digital transformation, automation, and sustainability are driving a revolution in how energy is found, extracted, and delivered. Stay ahead by understanding what's coming.
Technologies we cover:
Digital twins for offshore asset management
AI and machine learning in predictive maintenance
IoT sensors and real-time monitoring platforms
Subsea automation and remote operations
Carbon capture, utilisation, and storage (CCUS)
Hydrogen-ready infrastructure design
▶ SUB-SECTION 8: Case Studies & Real-World Applications
Intro
